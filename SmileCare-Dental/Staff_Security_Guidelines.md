# Core Security Guidelines for Clinic Staff
**Client:** SmileCare Dental Clinic - All Employees
**Prepared By:** Muhammad Ali Hussain, Cybersecurity Consultant
**Date:** October 2026

As healthcare providers, we are legally obligated to protect patient records from unauthorized access.
*   **The Clean Desk Rule:** Never leave patient files, billing information, or handwritten notes unattended on the front desk.
*   **Lock Your Screen:** Press `Windows Key + L` (or `Cmd + Control + Q` on Mac) every single time you step away from your workstation. It takes one second and prevents unauthorized access to the clinic's database.
*   **No External USBs:** Do not plug personal USB drives or unknown flash drives into clinic computers. Malware spreads rapidly through physical media.

## 2. Defeating Phishing (Email Security)
Attackers will try to trick you into handing over your password or downloading malware by pretending to be a trusted vendor, a patient, or clinic management.
*   **Verify the Sender:** Always check the actual email address, not just the display name. `SmileCare_Admin@gmail.com` is a fake; official emails will come from our internal domain.
*   **Pause Before Clicking:** Be suspicious of emails demanding "urgent" action regarding invoices, password resets, or patient complaints. Attackers use urgency to force mistakes.
*   **Confirm via Phone:** If you receive an unusual request for data or payment, call the person directly at a known phone number to verify it. 

## 3. Password Management
*   **Do Not Reuse Passwords:** Your clinic password must be unique. If you use the same password for your personal Netflix account and it gets breached, attackers will use it to access the clinic.
*   **Use the Password Manager:** You do not need to memorize complex passwords. Use the IT-approved password manager to generate and store your credentials securely.

## 4. Incident Reporting (The No-Blame Policy)
**If you click a suspicious link, download a strange file, or realize you made a mistake, contact the IT Operations Team immediately.** 
*   Do not try to hide it. 
*   Do not turn off your computer unless instructed.
*   **You will not be penalized for reporting a mistake.** Time is our most critical defense; the faster we know about a potential breach, the faster we can stop it from spreading to patient records.
